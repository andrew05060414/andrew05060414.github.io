# Editorial boundaries

Created: 2026-11-15 · Updated: 2026-11-15 · Version: 1.0

Primary audience: recruiters and engineers evaluating summer 2027 internship fit in AI infrastructure, LLM systems, and backend engineering. The secondary audience is developers interested in the owner's tools and contributions.

Lead with the person and concrete engineering work. Use first person, plain language, and evidence links. Distinguish the owner's projects, the upstream projects he contributes to, and dependencies he uses. Do not turn commit counts into claims about productivity, independence, or impact.

The design's numerical content was supplied as a 2026-10-28 snapshot. Preserve the date when reusing those values. Refresh a metric only from its owning source, with its scope and counting method, and update both languages together. A live clock is not a live activity feed.

Do not publish phone numbers, private repository links or code, internal addresses, local paths, credentials, conversation archives, or third-party personal information. A résumé remains available by email until the owner selects a public version.

The site's framework and hosting are implementation details, not the visitor's task. Contact links, project evidence, and the person behind the work should be findable within seconds. No automated social posts or job applications are part of this repository.
